# Contributing

Thank you for considering feedback or contributions to **Health Youpu / 健康有谱**.

This project is an open-source health literacy book for families. The goal is not to add as much content as possible. The goal is to make health judgment safer, clearer, and easier to use.

## What Helps

Useful contributions include:

- reporting factual errors, outdated links, or overconfident wording;
- improving warning-sign language and medical boundaries;
- recommending official sources, clinical guidelines, professional society materials, or high-quality reviews;
- improving reader flow, navigation, mobile readability, and practical templates;
- suggesting clearer family communication, doctor-visit preparation, or record-keeping workflows;
- adapting Chinese source pages into English without turning them into literal machine translations.

## What Does Not Fit

Please do not contribute:

- individual diagnosis, treatment, prescription, medication stopping, or personal medical advice;
- supplement, anti-aging, medical device, testing, or health-product marketing;
- copyrighted course text, OCR, screenshots, PDFs, audio, or long excerpts without permission;
- changes based only on a single news story, single study, or personal anecdote;
- wording that makes readers think the project can replace clinicians, emergency care, or individualized medical judgment.

## Medical Safety Standard

High-risk medical statements should be calibrated against official institutions, clinical guidelines, professional associations, or other high-quality public sources.

Be especially careful with:

- emergency warning signs;
- screening recommendations;
- medication boundaries;
- chronic disease thresholds and targets;
- mental-health crisis language;
- pregnancy/postpartum warning signs;
- children, older adults, immunocompromised people, and people with serious chronic disease.

When uncertainty exists, wording should preserve uncertainty rather than force a simple answer.

## How To Report Issues

Use one of the issue templates:

- **Reader feedback:** unclear wording, missing scenarios, navigation issues, or practical-tool feedback.
- **Medical boundary feedback:** wording that may delay care, overstate certainty, sound like diagnosis or treatment, or miss a high-risk boundary.

Please do not include medical records, ID numbers, phone numbers, addresses, insurance numbers, or other sensitive personal information.

## Chinese Source Edition

The Chinese edition is currently the source edition. English pages are preview adaptations unless otherwise marked.

If you are adapting a Chinese page into English:

- preserve the medical boundary;
- preserve the practical model;
- adapt examples for English-speaking readers where needed;
- do not expand medical claims beyond the Chinese source;
- keep links and metadata consistent.

# 贡献指南

感谢你愿意参与这个项目。这里最重要的不是增加文章数量，而是提高健康判断质量。

## 可以贡献什么

- 发现事实错误、表述过度或链接失效；
- 提出更清楚的危险信号或医学边界表达；
- 推荐官方机构、专业协会或高质量综述资料；
- 改善章节的读者场景、结构、语言和行动清单；
- 补充模板、清单和家庭协作流程；
- 帮助把中文源稿改写成英文，而不是逐字机器翻译。

## 不接受什么

- 个体诊断、用药、停药或治疗方案建议；
- 保健品、抗衰产品、健康设备营销；
- 未经许可的课程原文、OCR、截图、PDF、音频或长段摘录；
- 以单篇新闻、单篇研究或个人经验直接改写医学结论；
- 让读者误以为可以替代医生的内容。

## 证据优先级

高风险医学事实优先参考官方机构、临床指南和专业协会资料。数值阈值、筛查建议、疫苗建议、慢病目标和药物边界可能随指南更新，必须注明资料时间或保留复查空间。

## 勘误流程

发现问题时，请说明：

- 位置；
- 原表述；
- 问题类型；
- 建议修订；
- 依据来源。

勘误记录会保留在 `content/zh-CN/errata/`。

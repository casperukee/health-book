# Support

Thank you for reading or contributing to **Health Youpu / 健康有谱**.

This repository supports the book project and its website. It does not provide personal medical advice.

## If You Need Medical Help

Do not use GitHub issues, comments, or this repository for urgent health problems.

For chest pain, stroke-like symptoms, severe pain, fainting, breathing difficulty, severe bleeding, self-harm thoughts, or any situation that is worsening or feels unsafe, contact local emergency services, urgent care, or a qualified clinician.

## What You Can Ask Here

Good support requests include:

- a page is hard to understand;
- a handbook workflow is missing a common family scenario;
- a warning sign may be unclear or too weak;
- a page title, navigation path, or mobile layout is confusing;
- a link is broken or outdated;
- an English preview page does not match the Chinese source boundary;
- a sentence sounds like diagnosis, treatment, or individualized medical advice.

Please use the issue templates when possible:

- reader feedback for clarity, usability, and missing scenarios;
- medical-boundary feedback for safety, uncertainty, or care-seeking concerns.

## What Not To Share

Please do not post:

- medical records;
- ID numbers, addresses, phone numbers, insurance information, or photos of reports with personal information;
- private family health details;
- requests for diagnosis, medication changes, or treatment plans.

If an example is necessary, rewrite it as a general scenario and remove identifying details.

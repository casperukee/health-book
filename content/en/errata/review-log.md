# Medical Fact Review Log

This log records medical-boundary and source-review work for the English edition.

## Review Priorities

- Emergency and warning-sign pages.
- Mental health crisis and self-harm boundaries.
- Screening, test interpretation, and numerical thresholds.
- Medication, supplement, device, and consumer-health claims.
- Aging, caregiving, children, and family-record pages where delays or overconfidence could harm readers.

Each review entry should name the page, the question checked, the source used, the review date, and whether the wording changed.

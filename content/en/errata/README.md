# Errata

The English edition keeps corrections, medical fact review, and source-handling rules inside its own loop.

These pages are maintenance records, not medical advice. Safety-related issues take priority over style-only edits.

## Maintenance Pages

- [Corrections](corrections.md)
- [Medical Fact Review Log](review-log.md)
- [Source Handling Policy](source-policy.md)

## Priority

The highest-priority corrections are wording issues that could affect reader safety: warning signs, medication boundaries, screening language, mental health crisis resources, children and older adults, and claims about supplements, devices, tests, or longevity services.

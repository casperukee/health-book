# Source Handling Policy

The English edition uses sources to set boundaries, not to make the book sound more authoritative than it is.

## Policy

- Prefer official agencies, clinical guidelines, professional organizations, and patient-facing institutional materials for high-risk boundaries.
- Use news and single studies as leads, not as direct reasons to change medical advice.
- Record source choices in the English [Source Registry](../references/source-registry.md).
- If guidance may change, include a review date in the maintenance record.
- If a source is U.S.-specific, do not present it as universal for all English readers.

This book remains health education, not diagnosis, treatment, medication advice, or individualized screening guidance.

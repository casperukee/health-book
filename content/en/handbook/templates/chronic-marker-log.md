# Chronic Marker Log

> This template is not medical advice. Do not use it to diagnose yourself, change medication, delay urgent care, or decide treatment targets. Use clinician instructions, your own lab reference ranges, and local guidance.

[中文验收页](../../../en-review/zh-CN/handbook/templates/chronic-marker-log.md)

Use this for repeated numbers that need context: home blood pressure, glucose, A1C, lipids, kidney tests, uric acid, weight, waist, symptoms, medication changes, or follow-up results.

Do not turn health into a spreadsheet contest. The value is trend plus context.

## One Entry

```text
Date:
Where measured: home / clinic / lab / pharmacy / device
Marker or issue:
Result:
Unit:
Reference range or clinician target, if given:
Compared with last time:
Context: illness / poor sleep / alcohol / stress / pain / travel / exercise / medication change / missed doses / supplement change
Symptoms or function change:
Clinician's next step:
Follow-up date:
```

## Before A Visit

Bring one page, not a pile of screenshots:

```text
Main concern:
Most important trend:
Highest / lowest / most unusual result:
What changed around the abnormal result:
Medication, dose, missed-dose, or supplement changes:
Symptoms or daily function changes:
Questions for the clinician:
```

## What To Watch

| Pattern | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Trend | One unusual number may matter, but repeated movement is often more useful. |
| Combination | Blood pressure, glucose, lipids, kidney function, weight, and medicines can interact. |
| Context | Illness, stress, pain, sleep, alcohol, activity, and missed doses can change results. |
| Next step | A record is useful only if it helps clarify what to repeat, discuss, or change with a clinician. |

## Red Flags Still Come First

Chest pain, stroke-like symptoms, severe shortness of breath, fainting, major confusion, severe allergic reaction, uncontrolled bleeding, serious injury, self-harm risk, or rapidly worsening illness should not wait for a log entry.

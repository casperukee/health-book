# Handbook

This is the practical layer of Health Youpu. The main book explains why, how to judge, and where the boundaries are; the handbook helps a family take the next step in a concrete situation.

::: danger Check red flags first
If chest pain or pressure, possible stroke signs, severe breathing trouble, altered consciousness, uncontrolled bleeding, sudden severe pain, severe allergic reaction, poisoning, serious injury, self-harm or suicide risk, pregnancy/postpartum warning signs, or another possible emergency is already present, do not browse slowly through the handbook. Use emergency services, an emergency department, urgent care, crisis services, or another qualified local entry point first. You can open [Red Flags](playbooks/red-flags.md) at the same time to help family members state what is happening.
:::

## Pick Your Situation

| What you want to do now | Start here |
|---|---|
| The body or mind is already uncomfortable, and you are unsure whether it can wait | [Red Flags](playbooks/red-flags.md); if it is not an obvious emergency, use [Symptom Action Guide](playbooks/symptom-action-guide.md) or [Mental Health Check-In And Support](playbooks/mental-health-check-in.md) |
| You do not know whether to use emergency care, urgent care, primary care, or a specialist | [Emergency, Clinic, or Which Department?](playbooks/department-navigation-guide.md) |
| You need to review results, follow up, or see a clinician | First read [Checkup Markers](../book/part-1-healthspan-risk-and-markers/checkup-markers.md), then use [Common Checkup Markers](playbooks/common-checkup-markers.md) and [Doctor Visit Checklist](playbooks/doctor-visit-checklist.md) |
| A preventive visit or checkup is coming, and you are unsure what to discuss | [Before A Checkup](playbooks/checkup-planning-guide.md) |
| You want to prepare for family care | Start with [One-Page Family Health Card](templates/family-health-record.md#_1-one-page-family-health-card), then expand to [Family Health Record And Chronic Marker Log](templates/family-health-record.md) |
| Supplements, tests, devices, longevity services, or short videos are making the decision noisy | Use [Health Product Checklist](templates/health-product-checklist.md), then read [Supplement Evidence Traps](../book/part-4-health-information-defense/supplement-evidence-traps.md) |

## Minimal Family Workflow

Do not start from the full tool directory. Build one small loop that can be reused:

1. [Red Flags](playbooks/red-flags.md): agree as a family on what cannot wait.
2. [One-Page Family Health Card](templates/family-health-record.md#_1-one-page-family-health-card): make one small card for the person most likely to need care.
3. [Symptom Action Guide](playbooks/symptom-action-guide.md): for everyday symptoms, sort emergency, timely visit, and observation.
4. [Doctor Visit Checklist](playbooks/doctor-visit-checklist.md): when it is time to see a clinician, bring the timeline, tests, medicines, and questions.

Add other tools only when the matching situation appears:

- Before and after checkups: use [Before A Checkup](playbooks/checkup-planning-guide.md), [Common Checkup Markers](playbooks/common-checkup-markers.md), and the [Chronic Marker Log](templates/family-health-record.md#_2-chronic-marker-log).
- If the care entry point is unclear: use [Emergency, Clinic, or Which Department?](playbooks/department-navigation-guide.md).
- For mood, anxiety, stress, insomnia, or self-harm risk: use [Mental Health Check-In And Support](playbooks/mental-health-check-in.md).
- Before buying supplements, tests, devices, or longevity services: use [Health Product Checklist](templates/health-product-checklist.md).

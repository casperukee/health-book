# Handbook Preview

The handbook is the practical layer of Health Youpu.

The full English handbook is not complete yet. The preview currently includes:

- [Symptom Action Guide](playbooks/symptom-action-guide.md)
- [Emergency, Clinic, or Which Department?](playbooks/department-navigation-guide.md)
- [Before a Checkup](playbooks/checkup-planning-guide.md)
- [Doctor Visit Checklist](playbooks/doctor-visit-checklist.md)
- [Family Health Card](templates/family-health-card.md)
- [Health Product Checklist](templates/health-product-checklist.md)

These pages show the project’s intended style: short enough to use, careful enough not to replace medical care, and concrete enough to help families prepare better information.

## More Chinese Tools

The Chinese source edition already includes additional handbook pages for warning signs, common abnormal markers, mental health check-ins, chronic marker logs, and family health records.

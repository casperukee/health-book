# Handbook

The handbook is the practical layer of Health Youpu. Use it when a family needs a next action, a checklist, or a small record.

This handbook is not medical advice. If warning signs are present, use emergency services, an emergency department, urgent care, crisis services, or another local medical entry point before using checklists.

## A Minimal Family Workflow

Do not try to build a perfect health archive on day one. Start with four pieces:

1. Read [Red Flags](playbooks/red-flags.md) so the family agrees on what cannot wait.
2. Save the [Symptom Action Guide](playbooks/symptom-action-guide.md) for unclear everyday symptoms.
3. Make one [Family Health Card](templates/family-health-card.md) for the person most likely to need help.
4. Use the [Doctor Visit Checklist](playbooks/doctor-visit-checklist.md) before appointments, urgent-care visits, and follow-ups.

Add other tools when real situations appear.

## What These Pages Do Not Do

They do not diagnose, prescribe, recommend stopping medication, decide screening schedules, or replace emergency care or clinician judgment.

Their job is smaller and useful: help a family recognize when care should not wait, bring better facts into a visit, and keep key information from disappearing when stress rises.

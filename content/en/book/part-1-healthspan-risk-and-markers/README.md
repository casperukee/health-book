## How To Read This Part

Read [Healthspan and the Risk Curve](healthspan-and-risk-curve.md) first if you want the long-term frame: function, major risks, and family readiness.

Read [Checkup Markers Are Risk Language](checkup-markers.md) when a lab or checkup report has flags, reference ranges, or unfamiliar abbreviations.

If someone has warning signs right now, do not start here. Go back to [Medical Boundaries and Warning Signs](../medical-boundaries.md) or [Red Flags](../../handbook/playbooks/red-flags.md).

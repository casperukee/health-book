# Doctor Visit Preparation: Bring Facts Into The Room

> This page is not medical advice. If there are red flags, call emergency services or use urgent/emergency care first. Do not delay urgent care to make a perfect packet.

[中文验收页](../../../en-review/zh-CN/book/part-3-family-health-os/doctor-visit-preparation.md)

Clinic visits are short, emotional, and full of information. Preparation is not guessing the diagnosis. It is bringing the facts a clinician needs.

## Emergency Or Visit?

Emergency care is about time. Routine or scheduled care is about information quality.

If there is chest pain, possible stroke, severe breathing trouble, fainting, major confusion, uncontrolled bleeding, serious injury, self-harm risk, or rapid worsening, use emergency pathways. If this is a regular appointment, follow-up, checkup question, or non-emergency symptom, prepare the four packets below.

## The Four Packets

| Packet | What to bring |
| --- | --- |
| Symptom timeline | when it started, sudden or gradual, better or worse, triggers, relief, associated symptoms, function impact |
| Prior records | related labs, imaging, pathology, discharge summaries, prior diagnoses, prior plans, recent checkup results |
| Medications and allergies | prescriptions, over-the-counter medicines, eye drops, creams, supplements, herbs, alcohol/substance use, allergies and reactions |
| Three questions | the most important questions, in priority order |

## A Simple Opening

```text
I am here because of ____.
It started ____.
The main change is ____.
It affects ____.
I have tried ____.
My main question today is ____.
```

This is not a performance. It gives the clinician a starting map.

## Medication Honesty Matters

Bring photos or a list of everything actually used: prescribed medicine, nonprescription medicine, supplements, herbs, sleep aids, pain medicine, eye drops, injections, creams, and products started after online advice.

Include dose, frequency, why it is used, who prescribed it, missed doses, self-stopping, side effects, and allergies.

Do not say only "the white pill." Medication details can change diagnosis, side-effect interpretation, procedure planning, and safety.

## If You Accompany Someone

Good accompaniment is not taking over. Let the patient speak first when they can. Add facts they may forget. Record the plan. Confirm understanding before leaving. Respect privacy when the person needs to speak alone.

Before leaving, repeat back:

```text
Next we will ____.
The medicine/instruction is ____.
Results should be checked ____.
We should come back sooner if ____.
Follow-up is ____.
```

## After The Visit

Write the plan down within 10 minutes: main assessment, tests ordered, medication or care instructions, follow-up timing, return precautions, and who will handle the next step.

Save it in the [Family Health Card](../../handbook/templates/family-health-record.md#_1-one-page-family-health-card) or a shared record if the patient agrees.

You do not need to guess the diagnosis. Bring the timeline, records, medications, allergies, and three questions so the visit can start closer to the real problem.

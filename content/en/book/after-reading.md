# After Reading: A Few Moves To Keep

> This page is not medical advice. It only helps you carry the book's decision habits into ordinary family life. If red flags, worsening symptoms, a mental health crisis, or uncertainty about whether waiting is safe appears, use local emergency, urgent, crisis, or clinical care.

[中文验收页](../../en-review/zh-CN/book/after-reading.md)

You do not need to memorize every checklist in this book.

Keep the moves:

- When someone feels unwell, sort urgency before searching for disease names.
- When a report is flagged, look at trend, combination, context, and next step.
- When nothing is acutely wrong, protect the basics that make future risk easier to manage: sleep, movement, food, blood pressure, medications, vaccines, recovery, connection, and follow-up.
- When family care gets stressful, put facts, boundaries, next steps, and roles on the table.
- When a product, test, device, longevity package, headline, or short video creates urgency, ask what action it is trying to make you take.

This book does not turn a family into doctors. It helps a family bring clearer facts and better questions into the moments where doctors, clinicians, pharmacists, crisis teams, and emergency services are needed.

## Planned Structure

The English edition will follow the same structure as the Chinese source edition:

- Front Matter: how to use the book and where medical boundaries begin;
- Part 1: healthspan, risk curves, and checkup markers;
- Part 2: the body’s main risk lines;
- Part 3: a family health operating system;
- Part 4: health information defense;
- Handbook: playbooks, templates, references, errata, and updates.

The English version will be adapted rather than translated word-for-word.

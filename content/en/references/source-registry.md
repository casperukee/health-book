# Source Registry

The cross-language source registry is maintained at [`../../../shared/source-registry.md`](../../../shared/source-registry.md).

English chapters may add U.S.-first public sources when the adaptation needs local emergency numbers, crisis lines, screening authorities, health-record access, pharmacy and insurance context, or patient-facing U.S. care pathways. Those sources should also be synchronized back to the shared registry when they become part of the stable evidence base.

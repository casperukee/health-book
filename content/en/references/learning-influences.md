# Learning Influences And Acknowledgments

The English edition is an original U.S.-first adaptation of the Chinese book's family health decision system. It is shaped by public medical sources, patient-facing health education, caregiving practice, and the maintainer's broader learning history.

This page is for acknowledging influence without turning private notes, paid courses, OCR, screenshots, or source-specific structures into public content.

## Rules

- Do not publish paid-course text, OCR, screenshots, audio, images, or lecture-by-lecture summaries.
- Do not reproduce a private note structure just because it was useful during research.
- Do not make a page feel like a substitute for any paid course, book, or private collection.
- Use public medical sources to calibrate medical boundaries.
- Keep acknowledgments general unless a specific public source is directly used.

## Standard Note

This project is influenced by many books, courses, public medical resources, and personal synthesis. To respect copyright and reader safety, the public edition does not reproduce private materials or replace original sources.

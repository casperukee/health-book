# Official Medical Sources

This page lists the kinds of official or professional sources the English edition should use when a page depends on medical boundaries, screening, emergency care, public health, consumer protection, or family records.

## U.S. And International Anchors

- CDC for public-health topics, vaccines, infectious disease, emergency preparedness, and prevention materials.
- NIH and MedlinePlus for patient-facing explanations of diseases, tests, medicines, and body systems.
- NHLBI, NCI, NIA, NIMH, and other NIH institutes when a topic needs a more specific anchor.
- USPSTF for preventive services and screening recommendations.
- AHA, ACC, ADA, ACS, AAP, ACOG, AGS, and similar professional organizations when a chapter needs specialty boundaries.
- FDA, FTC, and NCCIH for supplements, devices, health fraud, product claims, and consumer health decisions.
- WHO or other international public-health sources when the English page should stay globally readable.

## How To Use This Page

Use this page as a source-finding starting point, not as a shortcut around source review. High-risk claims still need the specific page, guideline, review date, and wording checked before publication.

When a source is selected for an English page, record it in [Source Registry](source-registry.md).

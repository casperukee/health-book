# English Web Edition

**Health Youpu / 健康有谱** is an open-source health decision literacy book for families.

The English edition is now being rebuilt from the Chinese `v1.0.0` source edition as a **U.S.-first web adaptation**. It is not a sentence-by-sentence translation. The goal is to preserve the same family health decision system and medical boundaries while rewriting the examples, care pathways, units, source anchors, and family-collaboration scenes for English-speaking readers.

The current English pages should still be treated as a foundation preview, not as a complete English book.

## What This Edition Is For

This edition is for readers who want a calmer way to handle practical health questions:

- when to call emergency services, go to an emergency department, use urgent care, contact primary care, or watch and record;
- how to prepare useful facts before a visit instead of bringing guesses and scattered screenshots;
- how to keep medication lists, allergies, prior reports, discharge instructions, and family contacts from disappearing across portals and chats;
- how to support parents, partners, children, older relatives, or chosen family without turning care into control;
- how to read health claims, supplements, tests, devices, and longevity marketing without confusing confidence with evidence.

This project is **not medical advice**. It does not diagnose, prescribe, recommend stopping medication, create individualized screening plans, or replace emergency care or clinician judgment.

## U.S.-First, Still Portable

The first English adaptation is written for U.S. readers. That means it will use familiar U.S. care pathways and language where relevant:

- 911, emergency department, urgent care, primary care, specialist referral, telehealth, pharmacy, discharge instructions, and return precautions;
- preventive-care language, patient portals, medication reconciliation, insurance friction, network status, copays, deductibles, and prior authorization as planning context;
- U.S. lab-report conventions and units such as mg/dL and mmHg where appropriate;
- U.S. and international source anchors such as CDC, NIH, MedlinePlus, USPSTF, AHA/ACC, ADA, NCI, NIA, NIMH, SAMHSA, FDA, FTC, NCCIH, and WHO.

Readers outside the U.S. can still use the decision models, but they should map emergency numbers, care pathways, screening guidance, insurance rules, and public-health sources to their own country or region.

## Current Work Order

The English web edition will be rebuilt in stages:

1. **Safety and entry pack:** start here, medical boundaries, red flags, symptom actions, visit preparation, and the family health card.
2. **Checkups and risk language:** healthspan, risk curves, checkup markers, preventive-care planning, common abnormal markers, and chronic marker logs.
3. **Main risk lines:** metabolic, cardiovascular, sleep, mental health, cancer, specialty care, and other core body-risk chapters.
4. **Family health operating system:** records, visit preparation, parent conversations, elder care, privacy, consent, portals, and long-distance caregiving.
5. **Health information defense:** supplements, devices, tests, longevity marketing, health news, and consumer-protection boundaries.

The English reading edition should come later, after the web edition has been adapted, reviewed, and stabilized.
